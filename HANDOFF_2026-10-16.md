## BETA-02: Cover Flow misaligned and clipped (P1)

- **Root cause found by reading the code against your screenshots.** The blurred backdrop asked
  MusicKit for an `ArtworkImage` at a fixed 700 × 700. That view is rigid at the size it is asked
  for, so the Cover Flow `ZStack` became 700pt in its smaller dimension and every sibling was pushed
  by half the overflow: about 155pt to the right on a 390pt-wide portrait screen (selected cover,
  album text and the Tracks/Play buttons all land at the right edge, matching screenshot 01), and
  about 155pt down in landscape (carousel low and cut off, matching screenshot 02). The embedded
  presentation (Immersive Off) shares the same backdrop, so it broke the same way. The home screen's
  artwork pane avoids this because it frames and clips its artwork to the measured size.
- **Change:** `CoverFlowView.swift:136`, `coverFlowBackdrop(size:)` now requests the artwork at
  the view's larger side and frames and clips it to the view; `CoverFlowView.swift:83` pins the
  whole screen to the measured `GeometryReader` size and clips it, so no child can inflate the layout
  again. The backdrop no longer ignores the safe area; the screen's black background already covers it.
- **Verify, all four combinations** (Immersive On / Off × portrait / landscape), and record which
  one each screenshot shows:
  - The selected cover is centred and fully visible; neighbours may extend past the edges.
  - Album title and artist are centred and readable; the Tracks / Play bar (Immersive) sits fully on
    screen above the home indicator.
  - Browsing with the wheel and by drag, opening Tracks and returning to Covers, starting playback
    and returning, and exiting to the menu all work.
  - Rotating while on the screen keeps the carousel centred.
- **Not changed, still open:** immersive track rows are not tappable, so Play starts from track 1.

### Where the first build is most likely to complain

- `AppleMusicManager.insert(_:position:)` uses `MusicPlayer.Queue.EntryInsertionPosition` and
  `musicPlayer.queue.insert(song, position:)` / `insert(track, position:)`; and `jump(toEntryID:)`
  assigns `musicPlayer.queue.currentEntry`. These are documented MusicKit APIs that have not been
  compiled here. If the insertion position type is spelled differently in the iOS 27 SDK, fix the
  spelling only; do not change behaviour.
- `QueueEntryInfo` reads `entry.title`, `entry.subtitle` (optional) and `entry.artwork` from
  `MusicPlayer.Queue.Entry`, and keys entries by `String(describing: entry.id)`.
- `SongActionMenu` activates its own `FocusScope` in `onAppear`; the list re-activates its scope on
  dismiss. If a hold on a row ever leaves the wheel unresponsive, that hand-off is the place to look.
- `SeekScrubber.settleDelay` is a `Duration`; `Task.sleep(for:)` takes it directly.
